About the Loan Eligibility Checker System

The Bank Loan Eligibility Checker System allows users to quickly determine whether they qualify for different bank loans. Users simply enter their personal and financial information including age, monthly income, credit score and loan amount. The system evaluates these inputs using predefined eligibility criteria.

Based on the entered information, the system instantly determines whether the user qualifies for the selected loan type. It also calculates estimated monthly EMI and provides clear reasons for loan rejection if the eligibility requirements are not met.

This system supports multiple loan categories including personal loans, home loans, car loans, education loans and business loans. It is designed to help users understand their borrowing capacity and make informed financial decisions.
